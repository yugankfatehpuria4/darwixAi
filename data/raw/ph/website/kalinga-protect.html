Kalinga Protect Term Life

Kalinga Protect is a term life insurance plan offered through Bayanihan Bank branches. It pays a death benefit to your beneficiaries if the insured passes away during the coverage term.

Coverage

Coverage terms of 10, 15 or 20 years are available. The face amount (coverage) starts at PHP 500,000 and goes up to PHP 5,000,000. Entry age is 18 to 60 years old.

Riders

Accidental Death Benefit rider pays an additional amount equal to the face amount if death is due to an accident. Critical Illness rider pays 25% of the face amount in advance upon first diagnosis of a covered critical illness such as cancer, heart attack or stroke. Waiver of Premium rider waives future premiums if the insured becomes totally and permanently disabled.

Premium payment

Premiums can be paid annually, semi-annually, quarterly or monthly. Monthly mode requires auto-debit from a Bayanihan Bank savings or checking account. Premiums can also be paid over the counter at any Bayanihan Bank branch, through the Bayanihan Bank mobile app, or through accredited e-wallets.

Beneficiaries

You can name up to five beneficiaries and set the share of each one. Beneficiaries can be revocable, which you can change anytime, or irrevocable, which can only be changed with the beneficiary's written consent.

Bank referral

Bayanihan Bank depositors referred by a branch relationship officer get a free financial needs analysis. Policies bought through bank referral are serviced by both the branch and Kalinga Life customer care.

Kalinga Life Assurance is regulated by the Insurance Commission. Bayanihan Bank acts as a bancassurance partner. Deposits are not insurance products.
