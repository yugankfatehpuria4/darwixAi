# Payment, Grace Period, Lapse and Reinstatement: Kalinga Protect

Version 1.4 | Owner: Policy Servicing | Effective 2026-01-15

## Grace period

A grace period of 31 days is allowed from the premium due date for all payment modes. The policy stays in force during the grace period. If the insured passes away during the grace period, the death benefit is paid minus the unpaid premium.

## Lapse

If the premium is not paid by the end of the 31-day grace period, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy has no coverage and no benefit is paid for events after the lapse date.

## Reinstatement

A lapsed policy can be reinstated within 3 years from the lapse date. Reinstatement requires payment of all unpaid premiums with 6% annual interest and a signed health declaration. If the policy has been lapsed for more than 6 months, a medical exam may be required. Reinstatement is approved by underwriting and is not guaranteed.

## Change of payment mode

The policyholder can change the payment mode, for example from annual to monthly, on the next policy anniversary. Requests are submitted through Bayanihan Bank branches or Kalinga Life customer care.

## Premium amount

The premium of Kalinga Protect is level and does not increase during the coverage term. Riders added later are priced separately and increase the total premium.

## Hardship assistance

Policyholders who cannot pay because of job loss, illness or calamity can request a premium payment extension of up to 60 days, once per policy year, through a Kalinga Life customer care advisor. The request must be filed before the grace period ends. The bot cannot approve this request; it must schedule a callback with an advisor.

## Payment confirmation

Payments made over the counter or through the mobile app are posted within 1 banking day. Payments through e-wallets are posted within 2 banking days. The official receipt is sent by email and SMS.
