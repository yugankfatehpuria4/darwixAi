Super Top-Up Plan

Super Top-Up increases your health cover at a low premium. It pays once your total hospital bills in a policy year cross a fixed deductible, for example Rs 5 lakh. It can be bought with Family Shield or with an employer group policy.

Super Top-Up cover options are Rs 10 lakh, Rs 20 lakh and Rs 50 lakh, with a deductible of Rs 3 lakh or Rs 5 lakh. Customers who already have employer health cover often use Super Top-Up to protect themselves after they change jobs or retire.

Parents cover

Parents up to 65 years of age can be added to Family Shield. Parents above 65 years cannot be added to Family Shield and need a separate senior citizen plan, which is handled by a specialist advisor.

Optional add-ons

Maternity cover add-on

Maternity add-on covers delivery expenses up to Rs 50,000 for normal delivery and Rs 75,000 for caesarean delivery, after a waiting period of 2 years. It is available only on family floater policies with sum insured of Rs 10 lakh or above.

OPD add-on

OPD add-on reimburses doctor consultations, diagnostic tests and pharmacy bills up to Rs 10,000 per policy year.

Multi-member discount

A family discount of 10% on premium applies when two or more members are covered under the same individual policy. A long-term discount of 7.5% applies when the policy is bought for 2 years with single premium payment.
