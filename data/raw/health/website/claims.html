How to make a claim

Cashless claims

Cashless treatment is available at our network hospitals. For planned hospitalisation, inform us at least 48 hours before admission. For emergency hospitalisation, inform us within 24 hours of admission. Show your health card at the hospital insurance desk and the hospital will send the pre-authorisation request to us.

We aim to respond to a cashless pre-authorisation request within 1 hour and to final discharge approval within 3 hours.

Reimbursement claims

If you are treated at a non-network hospital, pay the bills and submit the claim within 30 days of discharge. Required documents are the claim form, original bills and receipts, discharge summary, investigation reports and a cancelled cheque for bank transfer.

Reimbursement claims are settled within 30 days of receiving all required documents.
