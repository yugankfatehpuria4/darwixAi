Family Shield Health Plan

One plan for your whole family. Hospital bills covered, cashless at 6,500+ network hospitals.

Who can buy Family Shield

The plan can be bought on an individual basis or as a family floater. A floater policy can cover the proposer, spouse, up to four dependent children and dependent parents or parents-in-law.

Minimum entry age for adults is 18 years and maximum entry age is 65 years. Dependent children can be covered from 91 days up to 25 years of age.

Coverage amount options

The cover amount (sum insured) is available in options of Rs 5 lakh, Rs 10 lakh, Rs 15 lakh and Rs 25 lakh. In a family floater, the entire coverage limit is shared by all insured members during the policy year.

What is covered

  • In-patient hospitalisation for at least 24 hours, including room rent, ICU, doctor fees and medicines.
  • Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 90 days.
  • Day-care procedures that need less than 24 hours of hospitalisation, such as cataract surgery and dialysis.
  • Road ambulance charges up to Rs 3,000 per hospitalisation.
  • Room rent up to single private room (no sub-limit) on sum insured of Rs 10 lakh and above. For Rs 5 lakh sum insured, room rent is capped at 1% of sum insured per day.

Waiting periods

Hospitalisation due to any illness is covered after an initial waiting period of 30 days from the policy start date. Accidents are covered from day one.

Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 3 years of continuous coverage. Specific listed illnesses such as cataract, hernia and joint replacement have a waiting period of 2 years.

No-claim bonus

For every claim-free policy year, the sum insured increases by 10%, up to a maximum of 50% of the base sum insured. If a claim is made, the bonus reduces by 10% in the following year.

Tax benefit

Premium paid for Family Shield may be eligible for deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, as per prevailing tax laws. Customers should consult their tax advisor.
