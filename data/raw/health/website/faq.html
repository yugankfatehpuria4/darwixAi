Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a medical check-up before buying the policy?

Applicants up to 45 years of age with no declared medical history do not need a pre-policy medical check-up. Applicants above 45 years, or anyone who declares a pre-existing condition, need a pre-policy health check-up at our cost at a partner diagnostic centre.

Is COVID-19 or other infectious disease hospitalisation covered?

Yes. Hospitalisation for infectious diseases including COVID-19 is covered like any other illness, after the initial 30-day waiting period.

Can I pay the premium monthly?

Yes. Premium can be paid annually, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly. Monthly payment is available only through auto-debit (e-mandate) from a bank account. Paying annually costs less overall than paying in instalments.

What happens if I miss my renewal date?

A grace period of 30 days is allowed after the renewal due date for annual premium, and 15 days for monthly premium. Coverage continues during the grace period but claims for treatment that starts during the grace period are paid only after the premium is received. If the premium is not paid within the grace period, the policy lapses and waiting periods restart on a new policy.

Can I cancel the policy if I change my mind?

Yes. You get a free-look period of 30 days from the date you receive the policy document. If you cancel within this period and have made no claim, we refund the premium after deducting stamp duty and the cost of any pre-policy medical check-up.

Can I port my existing health policy to Suraksha?

Yes. You can port an existing individual health policy from another insurer at renewal. Apply at least 45 days before your current policy's renewal date. Waiting periods already completed with your previous insurer are credited up to the previous sum insured.

Does Family Shield cover alternative treatments like Ayurveda?

Yes. In-patient AYUSH treatment (Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy) at a government-recognised hospital is covered up to the sum insured.

How many network hospitals do you have?

Suraksha Health has cashless tie-ups with more than 6,500 network hospitals across India. The current list is available on the website and in the mobile app.

Who can buy Family Shield?

The plan can be bought on an individual basis or as a family floater. A floater policy can cover the proposer, spouse, up to four dependent children and dependent parents or parents-in-law.

How do I make a cashless claim?

Cashless treatment is available at our network hospitals. For planned hospitalisation, inform us at least 48 hours before admission. For emergency hospitalisation, inform us within 24 hours of admission. Show your health card at the hospital insurance desk and the hospital sends the pre-authorisation request to us.
