# Sales Desk Operating Notes

Last reviewed 2026-05-10 by the sales operations team.

## Escalation desk

Calls that need a human advisor are transferred to the senior advisor desk, available Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 8 pm IST. Outside these hours, the bot schedules a callback for the next working day. For urgent escalations the desk lead is Priya Sharma (priya.sharma@suraksha-example.in, +91 98100 12345).

## Callback scheduling

Callbacks can be scheduled in one-hour slots between 9 am and 8 pm IST, Monday to Saturday. A callback must be scheduled within 2 working days of the original call. Customers can ask for a callback in Hindi or English.

## Quote delivery

Written quotes are sent by email or WhatsApp within 15 minutes of the call ending. A quote is valid for 30 days.

## Do not call

If a customer asks not to be called again, the agent must confirm the request, mark the lead as do-not-call, and end the call politely. No further sales calls can be made to that number.
