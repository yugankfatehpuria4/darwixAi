# Lead Qualification Rules: Family Shield

Version 2.0 | Owner: Underwriting | Effective 2026-04-01

## Rule Q1: Entry age

The eldest adult to be covered must be between 18 and 65 years of age (inclusive). A lead where the eldest adult is above 65 years is not eligible for Family Shield and must be referred to a specialist advisor for the senior citizen plan.

## Rule Q2: Children

Dependent children between 91 days and 25 years can be covered. Children above 25 years need their own policy.

## Rule Q3: Pre-existing conditions

A lead that declares a pre-existing condition (for example diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorder, heart disease or asthma) is not rejected by the bot. The lead is marked "needs underwriting review" and an underwriter callback is scheduled. The bot must never tell the customer that they are approved or rejected because of a medical condition.

## Rule Q4: Serviceable location

Family Shield is sold in all Indian cities where Suraksha Health has at least one network hospital. If the customer's city is not recognised, mark the city for manual verification; do not reject the lead.

## Rule Q5: Required fields for a qualified lead

A lead is "qualified" when all of the following are captured: members to be covered, age of the eldest member, city, pre-existing condition declaration (yes or no), and existing cover (yes or no). Preferred coverage amount is optional.

## Rule Q6: Human escalation

Transfer to a human advisor immediately when the customer asks for a human, raises a complaint about an existing claim, or asks a question the knowledge base cannot answer twice in the same call.

## Rule Q7: Prohibited statements

The agent must never guarantee claim approval, never quote a premium without the waiting-period disclosure, never offer a discount that is not in the product documents, and never give tax or legal advice beyond stating that Section 80D benefits may apply as per tax laws.
