# Family Shield Outbound Lead Qualification Script

Version 3.1 | Owner: Sales Excellence | Effective 2026-04-01

## Opening

Agent introduces themselves by first name, states they are calling from Suraksha Health Insurance about the health insurance enquiry the customer submitted, and asks if this is a good time to talk for five minutes.

## Call recording disclosure

Before collecting any personal details, the agent must say: "This call is recorded for quality and training purposes." If the customer does not agree to recording, the agent must offer a callback from a human advisor and must not continue qualification.

## Qualification questions

Ask in this order: who needs to be covered (self, spouse, children, parents), age of the eldest member to be covered, city of residence, whether anyone to be covered has a pre-existing medical condition, whether the customer has an existing health policy including employer cover, and the preferred coverage amount.

## Waiting period disclosure

Before discussing price or sharing a quote, the agent must tell the customer that illnesses are covered after a 30-day initial waiting period and pre-existing diseases after 3 years.

## Objection: the premium is too expensive

Acknowledge the concern. Explain that a single hospital stay can cost several lakh rupees and that the premium is fixed for the year. Offer to lower the coverage amount, suggest the family floater instead of separate individual policies, mention the 10% family discount for two or more members, and mention that monthly payment through auto-debit is available. Do not offer any discount that is not listed in the product documents.

Say: "I understand, budget matters. One hospital stay can cost several lakh rupees, and your premium stays fixed for the year. We can look at a lower cover amount, a single family floater instead of separate policies, a 10% family discount when two or more members are covered, or monthly auto-debit payments. Which of these would help most?"

## Objection: I already have health cover from my employer

Acknowledge that employer cover is valuable. Explain that employer cover usually ends when the customer changes jobs or retires, and the coverage amount is often shared or limited. Suggest a personal Family Shield policy so waiting periods are completed while the customer is still employed, or a Super Top-Up on top of the employer cover.

Say: "That is good to have. Employer cover usually stops when you change jobs or retire, and it is often shared or limited. A personal policy lets you finish the waiting periods while you are still employed, or you can add a Super Top-Up on top of your office cover."

## Objection: insurance companies reject claims

Acknowledge the concern. Share the published claim settlement record of 94% for financial year 2025-26 and explain the cashless process at network hospitals. Never promise that any specific claim will be approved.

Say: "I hear that concern a lot. Suraksha Health settled 94% of the claims it received in financial year 2025-26, and at our network hospitals the treatment is cashless, so the hospital deals with us directly. I cannot promise how any single claim will be decided, but the process is clear and documented."

## Objection: I need to think about it / call me later

Respect the request. Offer to send a written quote by email or WhatsApp and schedule a callback at a time the customer chooses. Do not pressure the customer.

Say: "Of course, take your time. I can send you a written quote by email or WhatsApp, and set up a callback at a time that suits you."

## Objection: I am healthy, I do not need insurance

Acknowledge it. Explain that accidents are covered from day one and illnesses after 30 days, and that buying while healthy means waiting periods are completed before they are needed and the premium is lower at a younger age.

Say: "That is the best time to buy. Accidents are covered from day one and illnesses after 30 days, and the waiting periods get completed while you are healthy, at a lower premium than later."

## Closing

Summarise what the customer told you, confirm the next step (quote, callback, or human advisor), thank the customer, and end the call.
